    About these figures

    • Source: Department for Transport and DVLA, vehicle licensing statistics, table VEH0120 (Great Britain). The file runs to . This page uses quarterly figures from 2013 onwards.
    • Each figure is a snapshot of vehicles on the last day of the quarter, not a count of new registrations.
    • SORN means Statutory Off Road Notification. The keeper has told DVLA the vehicle is not being used or kept on a public road.
    • Fuel types are grouped. Hybrid covers petrol and diesel hybrids. Plug-in hybrid covers petrol and diesel plug-in hybrids and range extended electric vehicles. Other covers gas, fuel cell and other fuel types.
    • To keep the page fast, the 80 largest makes are shown individually. All remaining makes are combined as "All other makes". Together the 80 cover about 97% of vehicles.
    • Percentage change compares the latest quarter with the same quarter a year earlier. Changes in shares are shown in percentage points.
